== Fragment (stated before the claims)

Two variables, `lo` and `hi`; naturals; expressions `lit`, `var`, `plus`;
commands `skip`, assignment, sequence, `ifz`, `while`; fuel-indexed big-step
semantics (`nothing` when fuel runs out). Volpano–Smith typing with a
program-counter label over any instance of the `Lattice` record
(`SecretTypes.Lattice.Record`), with the variables' labels a parameter
`label : Var → Carrier`. An observer is a down-closed set of labels; the
principal observer at `o` sees exactly the labels `ℓ ⊑ o`. The two-point
lattice `L ⊑ H` is the first instance (`Lattice.two-point`), with `lo`
labelled `L` and `hi` labelled `H`, and its public observer is the principal
observer at `L`. Not in the fragment: n variables, declassification,
procedures, concurrency, timing. Termination is the one channel the theorem
names as open.

N-6 supersedes its 2026-10-12 reading (ASSUMED by inspection: `⊔-L₁`, `⊔-L₂`,
`H ⊔ y = H` and decidable zero-testing), which was the two-point shadow of
the census. The candidate record in issue #9 also listed `⊥ ⊑ x` and
decidable `⊑`; the certificate shows neither is used. Decidability is
replaced by stability: agreement is an equation between naturals, which is
decidable, so the proof splits on whether `V` sees a branch label under
double negation.

== Separation from siblings (criterion 2 of the family bar)

* Noninterference is a property of *two* runs. Echo grades, tropical grades
  and occupancy grades are accumulations over *one* run; none of them can
  state N-1. That is the separation, and it is why this is a family and not
  a view. The converse connection — that a well-typed program's low
  observer has an Echo fibre that does not separate secret inputs — is a
  CONJECTURE, recorded in the README, not claimed here.
* A label is not an epistemic standpoint (README). The Kripke frame built
  for choreographic-types indexes knowledge by *views*; a label indexes
  *permission to flow*. Nothing here imports epistemic-types, deliberately.

== Open

* Termination-sensitive noninterference (close the named channel): needs a
  stronger typing rule for `while` on secret guards. Pre-registered:
  the falsifier is `secret-loop` above, which TSNI must refuse.
* Coalition observers: a union of down-closed observers is down-closed, so
  N-7 already covers it, but no union combinator or coalition instance is
  stated here. In a reader-set lattice a coalition is not a principal
  observer.
* Declassification; multiple variables.
