= secret-types — Proof Status
:revdate: 2026-11-09

Statuses are the closed vocabulary of `standards/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc`
§5 (PROVEN / PROVEN-WITH-AXIOMS / TESTED / ASSUMED / OPEN), with the matching
ledger tokens of `standards/docs/README-EXPLAINME-STANDARD.adoc` §5 in
link:docs/EXPLAINME.adoc#proof-ledger[the Proof Ledger]. Every PROVEN line names
its module. Every module is checked by `scripts/check.sh` under the §2.1
canonical invocation, `agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible`, and four
stricter flags (`--no-libraries --ignore-interfaces --double-check -W error`),
with no postulates. The flag set and the Agda pin live in exactly one place,
`scripts/proof-flags.sh`, so this file, the gate and the receipt cannot
disagree, and a flag-control fixture proves the flag is really in force.

Each receipt of a leg records the checker and its version, the invocation, the
`sha256` digest of the leg's inputs and the axioms that the run reported.
`scripts/grade-receipt.sh --check` fails when a leg's inputs change after the

grade. The script also refuses a claim that counts a leg without a row in the
correspondence record, and the reverse.

=== The §2.1 invocation, and how waiver W-1 closed

`PROOF-POLICY` §2.1 (dated 2026-11-01) names
`agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible` as the canonical invocation. It is
in force. Adopting it took two rounds:

* *2026-11-01.* Two constructor-injectivity lemmas were removed or re-expressed
  (`just-inj` deleted, `_≟_` through `pred`), and the two-point order became a
  computed predicate (`L ⊑ _ = ⊤`, `H ⊑ L = ⊥`), which removed every
  `L`/`H`-injectivity warning. Thirteen warnings remained, all
  `just`-injectivity in `Noninterference.Generic`, from matching `refl`
  against `exec … ≡ just t`. The flag was waived (W-1), on the belief that only
  a relational big-step semantics could remove them.
* *2026-11-09.* That belief was wrong, and was measured rather than argued. The
  thirteen clauses now read the run's result through `Prelude.just-inj`, defined
  by `cong` through a computing projection, which needs no unification rule. The
  statements, the semantics and every fixture are unchanged. Conversely, a
  relational semantics alone would *not* have been enough: a probe proving
  determinism of `c , s ⇓ t` raised 22 `UnsupportedIndexedMatch` warnings,
  because inverting two derivations of one command needs injectivity of `Cmd`'s
  constructors.

The warning is never suppressed. `tests/flag-control/IndexedMatch.agda` is a
correct proof that only `--cubical-compatible` refuses; `scripts/check.sh` fails
if it is ever accepted, so dropping the flag cannot pass the gate (tested both
ways). W-1 is kept, closed, in link:docs/proof-debt.adoc#waivers[`docs/proof-debt.adoc`].

=== What the grade is anchored to

`verification/receipts/agda_praxis.deed` is the proof receipt of
`PROOF-POLICY` §8.5.1 (profile `proof-receipt`). `scripts/grade-receipt.sh`
writes it, and CI checks it (`.github/workflows/proofs.yml`, job `receipt`).
It holds:

* `:repository` and `:source-commit`, the full commit that its current
  receipts name;
* `(trusted-base ...)`: the four postulates of `Agda.Primitive` (`Level`,
  `lzero`, `lsuc`, `_⊔_`) with their `BUILTIN` bindings. These are the only
  postulates in the prover-supplied modules of the import cone of
  `SecretTypes.All`. `scripts/check-trusted-base.sh` asks Agda for the cone
  (`--dependency-graph`), scans those modules with a comment-aware scanner
  (`scripts/scan-agda-postulates.awk`, with controls in `tests/trusted-base/`)
  and compares the result with the one list in `scripts/proof-flags.sh`;
* one `(claim ...)` per theorem, with its Agda, Rocq and Lean legs, and each
  leg's receipts with their lineage states (current, superseded, regressed).

The receipts come from a run ledger in `scripts/grade-receipt.sh`. Each row is
copied from the log of the CI run that printed it. The `receipt` job checks the
committed receipt, then writes the receipt for its own run and prints the rows
to record. The pinned reader (`readProofReceipt`, standards
`be39f900665fb7c042c05d1165da018adc1860f4`) derives each claim's grade. A
negative control runs with every check: the same receipt with a wrong `:pwg`
must be refused.

The reader's receipt fields have no slot for the axioms reported or for the
input digest, which §8.2 lists. The receipt records both as generated comments
beside each receipt, and `--check` gates the digest.

Proof debt, in the four dispositions the trusted-base policy requires, is
link:docs/proof-debt.adoc[] — all four empty, and enforced rather than asserted.

== Fragment (stated before the claims)

Variables of any type with decidable equality (the `Vars` record,
`SecretTypes.Vars`), a store giving each a natural; expressions `lit`,
`var`, `plus`; commands `skip`, assignment, sequence, `ifz`, `while`;
fuel-indexed big-step semantics (`nothing` when fuel runs out).
Volpano–Smith typing with a program-counter label over any instance of the
`Lattice` record (`SecretTypes.Lattice.Record`), with the variables' labels
a parameter `label : Var → Carrier`. An observer is a down-closed set of
labels; the principal observer at `o` sees exactly the labels `ℓ ⊑ o`. The
two-point development uses two variables, `lo` and `hi`
(`SecretTypes.Lang.Two`), and the two-point lattice `L ⊑ H`
(`Lattice.two-point`), with `lo` labelled `L` and `hi` labelled `H`; its
public observer is the principal observer at `L`. Not in the fragment:
declassification, procedures, concurrency, timing. Termination is the one
channel the theorem names as open.

== Separation from siblings (criterion 2 of the family bar)

* Noninterference is a property of *two* runs. Echo grades, tropical grades
  and occupancy grades are accumulations over *one* run; none of them can
  state N-1. That is the separation, and it is why this is a family and not
  a view. The converse connection — that a well-typed program's low
  observer has an Echo fibre that does not separate secret inputs — is a
  CONJECTURE, recorded in the README, not claimed here.
* A label is not an epistemic standpoint (README). The Kripke frame built
  for choreographic-types indexes knowledge by *views*; a label indexes
  *permission to flow*. Nothing here imports epistemic-types, deliberately.
* The four structures are kept apart by name in `docs/secret-types.adoc`
  (`#four_distinct_structures`): confidentiality label, epistemic standpoint,
  resource grade, Echo residue. No mapping is proved between any two of them,
  in either direction, and the separation above is the reason a relabelling
  could not preserve this theorem's statement — three of the four are
  single-run accumulations.

== Open

* Termination-sensitive noninterference (close the named channel): N-9 refutes
  it for the current typing rules and the explicit `Maybe` outcome observer.
  A stronger typing rule for `while` on secret guards or a revised observation
  model is a separate design. The existing `secret-loop` must be refused by
  any proposed termination-sensitive typing discipline.
* Coalition observers: a union of down-closed observers is down-closed, so
  N-7 already covers it, but no union combinator or coalition instance is
  stated here. In a reader-set lattice a coalition is not a principal
  observer.
* Declassification.
